Refund Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Summary: We offer a full refund within 7 days of your first payment if you are not satisfied. No questions asked.

14-Day Free Trial

All new accounts receive a 14-day Pro trial at no charge. You will not be billed until the trial ends, and you can cancel before then without owing anything.

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you subscribe and decide within 7 days of your first payment that GovCon MCP is not right for you, contact us at support@govconmcp.com and we will issue a full refund with no questions asked.

Subsequent Billing Periods

After the initial 7-day window, subscription payments are non-refundable. You may cancel at any time and retain access through the end of the current billing period. We do not provide partial-month refunds.

Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

  • Via the Paddle billing portal (link in your payment receipt email)
  • By emailing support@govconmcp.com

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Your account reverts to the Free tier (5 searches/day) at that point.

Billing Errors

If you were charged incorrectly (duplicate charge, wrong amount), contact us immediately and we will resolve it within 3 business days.

Contact

Refund requests and billing questions: support@govconmcp.com
