Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GovCon MCP ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our Service at govconmcp.com.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you register, we collect your email address and optionally your company name.

Usage data: We log API requests including timestamps, search queries, and tool invocations to enforce rate limits, detect abuse, and improve the Service.

Payment information: Billing is handled entirely by Paddle. We do not store credit card numbers or payment details — Paddle processes and stores all payment data under their own privacy policy.

Company profiles: If you use our profile-matching features, we store the capabilities, NAICS codes, and certifications you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To authenticate API requests and enforce subscription limits
  • To send transactional emails (API key delivery, payment receipts)
  • To detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized access
  • To improve the accuracy and relevance of search results

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Paddle — our payment processor, for billing and subscription management
  • Resend — our email provider, for transactional email delivery
  • OpenAI — search queries are embedded using OpenAI's API to enable semantic search
  • Railway — our hosting provider, where your data is stored and processed
  • Law enforcement or government authorities when required by law

4. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. Usage logs are retained for up to 90 days.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed API keys, and access controls to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email support@govconmcp.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

7. Cookies

Our landing page does not use tracking cookies or analytics. We may use essential session cookies for the registration flow only.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated by email to your registered address.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@govconmcp.com
